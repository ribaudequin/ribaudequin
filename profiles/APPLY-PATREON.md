# Patreon — Ribalinux → Bandua Studio

Page: `patreon.com/ribalinux` — handle is fixed and **cannot be changed**.
Created **June 2020**.

---

## Why this page changes at all

The page is a personal creator page for **Linux and BSD how-to videos**, asking for pledges toward
hardware. The YouTube channel no longer earns enough views to justify the production time, so it is
abandoned. The page has no reason to continue serving it.

**The decision: convert the page to Bandua Studio** (option B).

### The risk turned out to be zero

Before deciding, the numbers:

| | |
|---|---|
| Supporters | **20** |
| Monthly revenue | **$0.00** |
| Posts | 2 164 |
| Tiers | `Free`, `Alfa`, `Beta`, `Gamma`, `General Support` |

**20 supporters, $0 revenue** — every one is on a `Free` tier. Nobody pays. So the conversion costs
nothing: no revenue lost, no pledge broken, no promise to honour. This is unlike the Ko-fi page, which
has 13 supporters who actually donated.

The **2 164 posts stay** (owner's decision). They are the record of six years of work and deleting them
would be irreversible. A visitor will see years of Linux and BSD content — that is the honest history of
the page.

### Two Patreon-specific layout rules, and how they are handled

**The cover keeps its visual on the right.** Patreon puts the page title and About section on the left
of the cover, and advises against text on the cover at all. Measured on the generated file:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Mark position | x 1437–1962 of 2500 — **right half** |
| Left 42 % (the title area) | **0 content pixels** |
| Text on the cover | none, per Patreon's guidance |

**Tier cards are wide and short** (460 × 200). Patreon's guidance is one clear benefit, not detailed
text — so each card carries only the mark and the tier name.

> **A bug worth recording.** The first version estimated the text width from `len(label)`, so
> "Supporter" (9 characters) produced a wider text block than "Backer" or "Patron" (6). That pushed the
> Supporter mark off-centre and made the three marks **different sizes** — 196 px vs 167 px vs 164 px.
> The fix was to measure the rendered text instead of estimating it. All three marks now measure 88 px
> at the same vertical position. **Measure text; do not estimate it from character count.**

## Tiers — three levels

Replacing the current set, which mixed **two currencies** (USD and EUR), had inconsistent names
(`Free` on a $1 tier), and one untitled tier.

| Amount | Name | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| **$3** | `Supporter` | General support. Your name in the project's supporters list. |
| **$10** | `Backer` | As above, plus your name in the app's credits. |
| **$25** | `Patron` | As above, plus a mention in the project README. |

**USD only.** The page is already dollar-denominated and Patreon converts automatically.

### Why these values

- **$3** matches the Ko-fi page and the most-used preset on these platforms
- **$10** is the natural step up — ten times the minimum, still accessible
- **$25** replaces the old `Beta` tier at the top without being absurd

### What the tiers deliberately do NOT promise

| Not promised | Why |
|---|---|
| Early access | Contradicts "no accounts" — and releases are public anyway |
| Exclusive features | The software is MIT; there is no paid version |
| Priority support | Would create an obligation that cannot be guaranteed |
| Delivery dates | This is exactly what went wrong with the Ko-fi goal |

The brand makes open, local software. Tiers here are **recognition, not access** — promising access
would contradict the product.

### Accessibility check

The three cards differ by field colour, but colour is never the only signal — each carries its **name**.
Measured, including a deuteranopia simulation (the most common form of colour blindness):

| Pair | Normal | Deuteranopia |
|---|---|---|
| Teal vs Moss | 1.50:1 | **1.95:1** |
| Teal vs Deep | 2.08:1 | — |
| Moss vs Deep | 3.12:1 | — |

Teal and Moss do not collapse under deuteranopia, and the written name settles it regardless.
