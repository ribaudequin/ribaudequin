<h1 align="center">bandua studio</h1>

<p align="center">Things built to last.<br>by Marcelo Salvador</p>

---

I make small, simple tools that do one job well.

They run on your machine. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Local, private, yours.

## Projects

| Project | What it does | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [clavis](https://github.com/ribaudequin/clavis) | Encrypted notes for passwords, PINs, bank details and safe codes. | Electron · TypeScript |
| [epub-library-manager](https://github.com/ribaudequin/epub-library-manager) | Organise your EPUB library by series, with covers, metadata and reading progress. | Electron |
| [ValidadorPT](https://github.com/ribaudequin/ValidadorPT) | Offline validator for Portuguese NIF, IBAN and NIB. | Kotlin · Compose |

## What I care about

- **Local.** Your data stays on your device. Offline-first is the default.
- **Private.** No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics.
- **Simple.** One tool, one job.
- **Built to last.** Tools that still work in five years, without a subscription.
